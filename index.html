About



I am Jingdi Lei (雷京迪), a first-year Ph.D. student at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University.

I received my B.Eng. degree from Beijing Institute of Technology. My research focuses on large language models, multimodal large language models, and reinforcement learning, with an interest in expanding the boundaries of AI systems.

If you are interested in collaborating or chatting, feel free to email me at jingdi001@e.ntu.edu.sg.
